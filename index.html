Our Story

A letter is a promise you keep to your future.

Chitthi didn't begin with a grand business idea; it began in the quiet pages of a childhood diary. Growing up, I wasn't really good at making friends. While other kids had friend circles I often found myself all alone outside. And my notebook became my only refuge.

I would pour my feelings into those pages, but sometimes, a spark of hope would peek through. My younger self would write directly to the future me, asking the most important question a lonely kid could think of: "Did you finally make a lot of friends?"

Years later, reading those entries felt like a time machine. The words I wrote in the dark brought so much light to my present. It proved that sometimes, the words we need most are the ones we wrote years ago.

We built Chitthi to capture that exact magic. It's a way for everyone to send letters through time, to family, to friends, or even to yourself. Because a letter doesn't just deliver a message; it delivers a piece of who you were at the exact moment you wrote it.

Chitthi aims to arrive not when it's convenient, but when it matters most. On the days we wish we had someone's voice, we have their words.

— the Chitthi team ✦

Privacy Policy

At Chitthi, we understand that writing a letter is a deeply personal and vulnerable act. We treat your messages, recipient addresses, and contact parameters with the highest degree of respect, confidentiality, and security.

What We Store

  • Name, Address, Phone, Email
  • We DO NOT share your data with third parties
  • We store future letters securely
  • You can request deletion of your data at any time

Zero-Read Policy

Our team operates under a strict Zero-Read Policy for standard and scheduled letters. The printing process is automated and handled under strict confidentiality protocols. Senders can rest assured that their personal words are not parsed, reviewed, or shared with third parties.

Transactional Security

Chitthi does not collect or store your payment details. All financial transactions are completed via integrated eSewa and Khalti APIs. Your session credentials and wallet keys are securely processed directly on their encrypted servers.

"Your letters are safe with us. We never share your data."

Terms of Service

1. Scope of Service

Chitthi is a hybrid technology and stationery platform operated by Team Manka Kura in Nepal. We provide online letter composing tools, future delivery scheduling systems, and physical printing, packaging, and shipping coordination across Nepal.

2. Delivery & Scheduling Guarantee

For standard letters, we aim to deliver within the first month of purchase. For scheduled "Future Letters", we guarantee secure vault archiving of the digital draft and commit to physical doorstep dispatch in the weeks leading up to the designated date (up to a 5-year maximum).

3. Word Limit & Calligraphy Surcharges

Standard letters are limited to 2,000 characters. Typing beyond this limit adds an automatic NPR 20 fee, up to a maximum of 4,000 characters total.

4. Prohibited Content

Users are strictly prohibited from writing letters that contain threatening, defamatory, harassing, or illegal content. We reserve the right to flag and cancel any order that violates local laws of Nepal, without issuing a refund.
